**Chrome in the Lands Between v0.1** - the bench build.

The first build that runs inside the game: the chrome HUD and the ripperdoc bench, with all 37
pieces of chrome browsable, tiered and fittable. It writes nothing to the game's own numbers yet -
deliberately, so a first run can only fail on loading.

**Install:** unzip into your Mod Engine 2 folder, then launch Elden Ring offline through Mod Engine 2.
Built for Elden Ring 1.17.1 (eldenring.exe 2.7.1.0). Press **F** in game.

The effects - bullet time, the arm cannon, the quickhacks, the passives - are the next build.
